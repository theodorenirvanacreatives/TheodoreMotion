About

Motion designer and creative director based in Bali — five years deep in live events, brand campaigns, and AI-driven production.

I work at the intersection of traditional motion craft and emerging AI tools — using After Effects, Blender, and a stack of generative video platforms not as novelties, but as production instruments integrated into real client deliverables.

Day-to-day I run event visuals for conferences and brand activations. In parallel I take on freelance work for brands that want visuals with an edge — dark, cinematic, minimal text, controlled motion.
